Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains, in general terms, how Travel Thrive handles personal information shared with us when arranging travel.

Information We Collect

When you reach out to Travel Thrive by phone, email or in person to book flights, buses, trains, hotels or holiday packages, we may collect personal information such as your name, contact details, travel document or passport details, and itinerary preferences, as needed to arrange your travel.

This Website Does Not Collect Data

This website is a static, informational site with no forms, accounts or backend systems. It does not collect, store or transmit any personal information from visitors. Any information Travel Thrive holds about you is provided directly by you through phone, email or in-person conversations.

How We Use Your Information

Personal information you share with us is used only to arrange your bookings and travel services, including tickets, hotel reservations, visas, insurance and related arrangements.

Sharing With Third-Party Travel Providers

We share your information only with the third parties necessary to fulfil your booking, such as airlines, railways, bus operators, hotels, insurers and visa authorities. Travel Thrive does not sell your personal information to third parties.

Data Security

Travel Thrive takes reasonable care to protect the personal information you share with us. As this website does not collect or store data, no online payment or personal data is transmitted through it.
