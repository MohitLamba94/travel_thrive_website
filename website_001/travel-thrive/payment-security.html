Payment Security

Please read carefully before making any payment

Accepted Payment Modes

Travel Thrive accepts payment through cash at our office, bank transfer, and card payments made directly with us or with the relevant airline, railway, bus operator, hotel or service provider. This website does not have a payment gateway or backend, and no card or banking details are ever entered on this site.

Payment Confirmation

Once a payment is received, Travel Thrive issues a receipt or booking confirmation for your records, either in person, by email, or over WhatsApp/phone as agreed with our office. Please retain this confirmation until your travel is complete.

Verifying Official Contact Channels

  • Always confirm you are dealing with our official office, phone numbers, or email address before making any payment.
  • Our official contact details are +91 99996 87120, +91 11 4564 2929, and contact@travelthrive.com.
  • If in doubt about the authenticity of a communication claiming to be from Travel Thrive, please call our office directly to verify before proceeding.

Unofficial Channels and Unsolicited Links

Travel Thrive does not request payments through unofficial channels, unsolicited links, or third-party payment collectors. We will never ask you to make a payment through a link sent unexpectedly by SMS, email, or social media. Please treat any such request with caution and verify with our office before acting on it.

Contact

For any questions about payment security, please contact us at contact@travelthrive.com or call +91 99996 87120.
