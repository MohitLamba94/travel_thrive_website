Privacy Policy

Please read carefully

Information We Collect

Travel Thrive only collects information that you voluntarily share with us when you contact our agency by phone, email, WhatsApp, or in person at our office — for example, your name, contact number, email address, and travel preferences shared while making an enquiry or booking. This website itself contains no forms, no cookies-based tracking, and does not collect any data through the site.

How We Use Your Information

Any information you share with us is used solely to process your travel bookings, arrange tickets and related services, and respond to your enquiries. We do not use your information for any purpose beyond assisting with your travel arrangements.

Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Where necessary to complete a booking — for example, sharing traveller details with an airline, railway, bus operator, hotel or insurer — we share only the information required to fulfil that booking.

Data Retention and Security

We retain booking and contact information only as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services and maintain records as required. We take reasonable care to keep any information you share with us secure.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about how your information is handled, please contact us at contact@travelthrive.com or call +91 99996 87120.
